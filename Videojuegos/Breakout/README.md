Welcome to firefly\!  
In this game you (the player) are tasked with saving a firefly from starvation or certain death.

The objective of the game is to fulfill three levels / meals. You complete one level when all the leaves are destroyed on the screen by the firefly. The leaves are the bricks at the top of the canvas underneath the counters of lives and levels. The firefly eats a leaf by making contact with said object. 

Unfortunately the firefly has no stamina left so you need to help it bounce to its food. Using the arrows in your keyboard (left pointing arrow to move left, right pointing arrow to move right) you must control a leaf to bounce the firefly to its objective and avoid the light.  The leaf you control is at the bottom of the screen above the time counter and leave’s eaten counter. 

Remember that fireflies are sensitive to light. So everytime it makes contact with the light at the bottom of the screen (bottom wall marked in cream) the firefly will reset to its original position and lose a life. You need to complete all levels before the sunrise as the sunlight will directly kill the firefly. This is the time limit shown at the bottom of the screen.

To start the game or run click the space key in your keyboard, the firefly will move in a random direction. 
Good luck :)
